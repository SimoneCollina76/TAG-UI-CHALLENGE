Synopsis

In 1952 New York, ambitious and self-centered Marty Mauser dreams of becoming the world’s greatest table tennis player. Struggling with money, family pressure, and a reckless affair, Marty fights to reach the world championships in Japan, hoping victory will bring fame, fortune, and recognition. But as his determination grows, his impulsive choices threaten to destroy everything around him.

Timothée Hal Chalamet

December 27, 1995 · New York, New York, United States

Timothée Hal Chalamet was born in Manhattan to a family deeply connected to the world of art and entertainment.

Son of Nicole Flender and Marc Chalamet, he has Russian, Polish, French, and English Jewish origins. He is the brother of actress Pauline Chalamet and the nephew of screenwriter Harold Flender.

Raised in New York, he started at a young age between commercials and theater. He made his film debut in 2014 with Interstellar and Men, Women & Children.
The recognition came with Call Me by Your Name, which earned him an Oscar nomination for Best Actor.

Today he is considered one of the most talented and influential actors of his generation.
